Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: November 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information for our application, Tic Tac Toe.

We Do Not Collect Your Personal Data

Our application, Tic Tac Toe, does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information from you.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

For the app to function as intended, the following information is saved locally on your own device using the app's internal storage:

  • Player names and their corresponding win scores.
  • Your chosen preferences for the app theme, AI difficulty, and sound settings.

This information remains on your device and is never sent to us or any third party. We have no access to this data. You can clear this data by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
